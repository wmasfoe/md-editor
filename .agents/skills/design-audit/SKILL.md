# Design Audit Skill

## Core Philosophy
- Simplicity is architecture: Eliminate everything that doesn't serve the user.
- Consistency is non-negotiable: Pixel-perfect precision across states and components.
- Hierarchy drives everything: One dominant focal point per region.
- Apple HIG alignment: Respect materials, depth, physics-driven animations, and accessibility.
